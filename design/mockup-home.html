Pinning to a tag does not pin the code.

When your workflow says uses: owner/action@v4, the label stays put while the code behind it can change overnight. GitHub keeps no record of where a tag used to point.

In March 2025, 346 tags of tj-actions/changed-files were moved to one malicious commit. In March 2026, 76 of 77 tags of aquasecurity/trivy-action were. Refledger is the record that would have shown both, the moment they happened.
